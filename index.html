Do I still need my regular atta?

Yes, and that's the idea. ProGrain is an add-in, not a replacement. You knead one sachet into your usual atta, so you keep the brand and the taste your family already likes.

Will it change the taste or texture of my roti?

We built the recipe so your roti still rolls, puffs and tastes like roti. Taste is personal though, so we'll share sampling details with the waitlist before launch.

How much protein does it add?

Our target is about 8 g per roti, roughly three times a plain whole wheat roti. That is a target until independent lab results are in. We'll publish the full nutrition panel once they are.

Is it safe for my dietary needs?

ProGrain contains wheat (gluten) and soy, so it is not suitable if you are allergic to either. The final allergen list will be on the pack. If you have diabetes, kidney conditions or any medical diet, please check with your doctor first.

We're finishing licensing, lab testing and packaging. Waitlist members hear first, and we'll share a date as soon as it's firm.
